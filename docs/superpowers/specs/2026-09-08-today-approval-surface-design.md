# Today Approval Surface Design

## Intent

Make the Home approval surface feel like a calm product control for artist teams. The reference is Perplexity's restraint: clear hierarchy, neutral surfaces, precise spacing, and no decorative accents competing with the decision.

## Approved design

- Remove the purple left inset and tinted primary-row fill.
- Keep one neutral outer surface with a quiet hairline border and restrained shadow.
- Expand approval details directly beneath the selected row. Do not place a second rounded card inside the surface.
- Use the permission action as the expanded heading, followed only by concrete details when they exist and the two decisions.
- Keep `More from Desk` as a low-emphasis footer row.
- Add explicit space between the Activity label and unread badge.
- Preserve keyboard focus, dark mode, mobile layout, loading, error, approve, and reject behavior.

## Acceptance

The Today surface contains no left accent border, the expanded approval has no nested card geometry, the actions remain immediately understandable, and the Activity count does not touch its label.
